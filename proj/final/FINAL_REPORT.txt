  1. Reuters attribution: Reuters' own article was not reachable; the "could not independently verify" line is reported by Arabic
     outlets citing Reuters and by Tawassoul/Anadolu. Shown as a chip on the Sky News headline card.
  2. al-Bukhaiti: narration says he rejected the reports, cited "deterrent capabilities" and warned of strikes on vital Saudi
     facilities. No verbatim text was found (only paraphrases + a 4 Oct tweet about "leave before it's too late").
     Shown as a points card «بحسب ما نُقل عنه» with chip «لم نعثر على النص الحرفي للتصريح». No photo used (Commons only has
     ALI al-Bukhaiti — a different person — deliberately NOT used).
  3. Aerial city footage (hook, 04:38, 10:22, 14:36) is from Al-Masirah's 5 Oct report; the city is not named on screen
     (labelled «لقطات جوية بثّتها قناة المسيرة») because the location could not be independently confirmed.
  4. Almasdar clip 32922 labelled «المخا · بحسب الناشر» (publisher's caption).
  5. Guest figures shown as the guest's: distances 36/34/30 km (straight-line OSM distance Mokha–Dhubab town ≈ 45 km), "8 km
     from Mokha" (chip «غير مؤكد»), Harad→Kahbub redeployment (chip «لم يتأكد»), "Alimi requested international help" (chip «بحسب الضيف»).
  6. Mafraq al-Mokha / Khalid bin al-Walid camp pin is approximate (labelled «تقريبي»); al-Birin shown as a text chip, not a pin,
     because OSM's "al-Birin" (Sabir al-Mawadim, south of Taiz) conflicts with the publisher's "west of Taiz".
  7. Houthi-media material always carries «رواية إعلام الحوثيين»; government material «رواية القوات الحكومية» / «مشاهد بثّتها القوات الحكومية».
